BookSwap (2024)

I created a book swapping platform designed to allow user to trade books with others!

I origninally started this in 2021 using Javascript but converted it to typescript in 2024 to encourage me to use the best practices (since it's more enforced).

This came from my love of books + the traction reading has been getting over the past few years! The book community is so generous and welcoming!

I connected the app with a few external services to make everything work smoothly:

  • Auth0 handles secure user authentication and authorisation, enabling users to create accounts, log in, and manage their listings through token-based access control.
  • A SQL database stores all the listing details (like users, book titles, genres, descriptions, and availability). I'm working on getting the database setup version-controlled so changes can be tracked over time.
  • Cloudinary stores the book cover images that users upload, and makes sure they load quickly on the site.

It isn't live yet and I was very much focussing on the functionality before the design (I am a backend girl at heart), but I am pretty proud of this one!

AT-Hop App (2021)

Disclaimer: This was part of a friends personal project - public transport apps have developed since so the ideas here are a bit outdated but cast yourself back to 2021 pls thnku


A friend of mine who is an industrial designer had a side project/idea to improve the way we use AT Hop cards in Auckland. For those who don't know, in Auckland we use Hop cards to pay for rides on public transport.

Currently, if you don't have enough money on your card, you have to get off the bus/train and find a top up booth. If you try to transfer money to your card online it takes about 48-72 hours to appear. This means if you run out of credit you will have to wait for the next bus/train/ferry etc. after you top up elsewhere, which can be very frustrating.

My friend had the idea creating an app that allows people to use their phone to tag on instead of the Hop card, similar to the way Apple Pay can be used for paywave.

The app will also allow users to transfer money immediately if their balance is low, so they don't need to miss the bus anymore.

He created amazing visuals for the app and asked me to bring it to life!

I had never made an app before or used React, so it was exciting for us both when I was able to do it!
